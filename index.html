Privacy Policy

Clubhouse games · Effective October 12, 2026

This policy covers the Clubhouse games apps for young children, made by Clubhouse Designs (“we”). Right now that is Dino Hatch on Google Play. New games will be added to this page before they are released.

The short version

  • We don’t collect any information about you or your child.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no chat.
  • Game progress stays on your device.
  • Purchases are optional, are made only by a grown-up behind a parent gate, and are handled by Google Play.

Information we collect

None. Our apps don’t ask for names, email addresses, photos, voice, location, contacts or any other personal information. They don’t create accounts, don’t use advertising IDs or other device identifiers, and contain no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. Nothing is ever sent to us.

What stays on your device

To remember where your child left off, the app saves its progress (for example, which dinosaurs have hatched and what they’re wearing) and a note of what has been unlocked. This is kept only in the app’s private storage on your device.

The app also reads the device’s date to pick seasonal decorations. The date isn’t stored or sent anywhere.

To delete this information, use Reset progress in the app’s grown-ups’ area, or uninstall the app, which removes everything it stored.

Purchases

Some content can be unlocked with an optional in-app purchase. Purchases can only be started from the grown-ups’ area, which is behind a parent gate. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see your payment details. The app checks with Google Play which purchases have been made, so it can unlock that content and restore it after a reinstall. This is the only time the app uses the internet. Google’s handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

App store statistics

Google Play gives developers anonymous, combined statistics, such as the number of installs and, from people who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google, anonymous crash reports. Google collects these under its own policies, not through our app, and they don’t identify you or your child.

Children’s privacy

Our games are made for young children. We don’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Nothing a child can reach in our apps links out of the app, shows ads or lets them talk to other people, and nothing a child can reach spends money.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above before the changed app is released.
